<Callout title="A long grind">
  Every ingredient drops in 1 kill of 10, so one potion is about 80 kills across 6 zones, and the amulet takes about 30 potions on average. Each drop goes to the player with the last hit, so a party only helps you kill faster — every player still needs their own 80 kills per potion.
</Callout>

<Section title="Before you start" lead="Two things that change the loop.">
  <Cards>
    <Card title="Scrolls of Escape">
      C1 has very few teleports, so you run between zones. Carry several SoE: at least one after Windy Hill and one after the Gorgon Flower Garden.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Level 35+">
      The quest starts at 30, but ants and tyrants are level 35–36 and the Turak Bugbear Warrior is 33. The Secret Keeper Tree is 47 but not aggressive.
    </Card>
  </Cards>
</Section>
