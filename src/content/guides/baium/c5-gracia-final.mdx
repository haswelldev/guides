<Callout title="Everyone runs their own quest">
  Every player needs their own powerstone, keys and fabrics, but a party still makes it much faster. **Ark guardians:** only one spawns at a time, so take turns — one player opens the ark, the party fights, the owner lands the last hit. **7th floor:** hits count per player, so the whole party can punch the same paralyzed mob at once. **Angels:** each kill gives a fabric to only one party member with the quest, so take turns, 1–2 players at a time.
</Callout>

<Section title="Before you start" lead="Two things that change the run.">
  <Cards>
    <Card title="A party for the arks">
      2–3 people make the arks quick. Supports need a weapon for the last hit.
    </Card>
    <Card title="A party for the 7th floor">
      A Necromancer to paralyze, plus attack speed: Bladedancer, Prophet, and Prophecy of Fire or Wind if you can get it.
    </Card>
  </Cards>
</Section>
