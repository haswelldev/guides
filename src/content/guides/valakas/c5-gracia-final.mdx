<Callout title="Alone or in a duo">
  Each kill gives an ore to only one party member with the quest, so in a big party everyone waits a long time for their 50. Do it alone or with one support. If you came as a group, **split up** and farm in different spots — everyone gets their ore faster.
</Callout>
