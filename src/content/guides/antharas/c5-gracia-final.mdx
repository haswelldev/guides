<Callout title="Tested on L2Reborn Signature">
  You can farm the items *before* you talk to the NPC who asks for them. The quest only needs to be started. This is why the whole farm part can be done first.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Bring 3 people or more">
  Solo works, but it is slow and boring. Everyone needs their own items. **Normal mobs: about 1 mob per person.** Example: 6 people want the Marsh Stalker Horn, so you kill 6 Marsh Stalkers. **Spawn mobs are better for a group** — one pull spawns several quest mobs, so several people get the item at the same time. Every hunt step below says which type it is.
</Callout>

<Section title="Before you start" lead="Two things that change the route.">
  <Cards>
    <Card title="Up to 7 SoE">
      One to leave each farm zone. Most of them bring you to Aden. The one from Forest of Mirrors brings you to Hunters Village — that is why this zone is last. Without noblesse you use one less, because you run to Giant's Cave on foot.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Noblesse port">
      With noblesse, use the Anghel Waterfall port from Aden. It puts you next to the Harit camps, much closer than the normal Forest of Mirrors port.
    </Card>
  </Cards>
</Section>
