The standard walkthrough ping-pongs: talk to a watchman, fly out, farm one drop, fly back, repeat — four times over. This route farms everything first in one Aden-hub circuit, then pays out the marks in geographic order. Same quest, same items, half the town trips.

26standard steps 18stops here

Walked and verified on L2Reborn Signature

The reordering works because drops register while the quest is simply active — you do not need to have spoken to the watchman who wants an item before the item will drop. That is what lets the whole farm circuit happen up front, and it is confirmed in-game here.

Bring at least three

Run this in a group of three or more — solo is possible and thoroughly annoying. Credit is per character, so everyone needs their own copy of every item, but how much that costs the group depends on the step. Ordinary mobs are roughly one kill per person: six people want the Marsh Stalker Horn, six Marsh Stalkers die. The spawner steps are where a group pays off — one trigger drops several quest mobs at once, so several people collect in parallel. Each hunting step below says which kind it is.

Before you undock

Both of these shape the route below.

Up to 7× Scroll of Escape

One out of each farm zone. Most return you to Aden; the one from Forest of Mirrors drops you in Hunters Village — which is why that leg runs last. Without noblesse you burn one fewer: Giant's Cave is reached on foot from Forsaken Plains.

Noblesse shortcut

If you are noble, take the Anghel Waterfall port out of Aden instead of the Forest of Mirrors gatekeeper run — it lands you noticeably closer to the Harit camps.

Forest of MirrorsHarit Lizardman campsHunt

Noblesse: take the Anghel Waterfall port out of Aden — the Harit camps are right at the landing point. No noblesse: gatekeeper to Forest of Mirrors and run in from the port. Kill Matriarchs and Shamans to trigger Harit Lizardman Zealots — three per spawn, so three people collect at a time. The efficient shape here is to break the group into cells of one DD and two supports and work separate camps in parallel, rather than stacking everyone on one pull. Run this leg last on purpose: the SoE out of here lands in Hunters Village, which is the next stop.

11
Windy Hillout of Gludin VillageHunt

The jewel is not a kill, it is a switch. Beat it below 50% HP; from then on a single hit hands the fragment to whoever landed it — supports and buffers walk up and punch it bare-handed for their own copy, no damage contribution needed. Get the whole party tapping it at once; that is the fastest fragment on the sheet. The jewel also throws out Jewel Guardian Mara in numbers, so there are fangs enough to go round, but they are ordinary kills: nine collectors means at least nine guardians down. SoE back to Gludin.
